# Guardrails: the checks and safeguards in place

These protect the quality of every reel and the safety of the live channel.
Each one exists because of something that really happened or nearly did.

## 1. Checks while a reel is built

The builder **refuses** to make a reel if any of these fail:

| Check | Why |
|---|---|
| The reel is 60 seconds or longer. | The channel's hard rule is under 60 seconds. |
| A subtitle line is too long for one line. | Two-line subtitles cover the speaker and read badly on phones. |
| A title is too long for one line. | Same reason, at the top of the frame. |
| The cut crosses a glitch in the source video. | One talk had a 1.6-second "Missing File" card that reached a reel. |
| The Kannada description has broken letters. | Wrong vowel signs, or a letter from another script, slip in when typing. |

## 2. Checks before a reel is released

| Check | How |
|---|---|
| Size and length | Vertical 1080 x 1920, under 60 seconds. |
| Spelling | English against a dictionary; Sanskrit and Madhwa names against a list of confirmed spellings. Unknown names are flagged, never guessed. |
| Clean ending | The quiet after the last word is measured on the finished reel, so it never ends mid-word. |
| Every frame | The finished reel is scanned frame by frame for glitches. A single sample frame once missed one. |
| A look | A few frames are viewed: title, subtitles and watermark readable, nothing overlapping, the speaker's face clear. |
| A listen | The start and end are heard. Passing numbers alone have been wrong before. |
| Content | The moment ends on a complete thought and attacks no one. |

## 3. Safeguards on the live channel

- **Deleting, replacing or hiding** a published video happens only when the
  owner names that video. Its id and title are stated first.
- **Deleting is permanent**, and replacing is not a swap: the new video gets
  a new link and the old views are lost. This is said every time.
- **One reel per slot.** Two reels are never booked into the same slot, and
  nothing is published at once unless the owner asks. Releasing many reels on
  the same day cost them most of their views.
- **A record of booked slots** is kept, because YouTube doesn't always show a
  reel that was scheduled minutes ago. Without it, two reels once landed on
  the same day. Deleting a scheduled reel frees its slot in the record.
- **A review reel first** for a new speaker or a new look. The rest are made
  only after the owner approves it.
- **No large batch of uploads** unless the owner asked for those reels.
- **Instagram is never automated.**

## 4. Safeguards on credit and content

- The speaker is credited in every reel. If the video doesn't name the
  speaker, the owner is asked.
- Verses are shown by their meaning. A word of a verse is never guessed.
- Passages criticising other traditions, communities or people are skipped.

## 5. Safeguards on files and logins

- The YouTube login files are never shown, shared or published. If one is
  ever exposed, it is reset.
- Files are never deleted in bulk with wildcards, and the full list is shown
  before anything is deleted. Downloaded talks and finished reels are kept.
- If something looks missing, it is checked before assuming it's lost. Once,
  "missing" files had simply been removed by the owner.
- Videos, talks, scripts and logins stay on the owner's computer, never in
  this repository.

## 6. Safeguards on the rules themselves

- A style rule changes only with the owner's agreement.
- A one-off instruction does not become a standing rule without asking.
- A reel outside the current task is not rebuilt or re-uploaded; it is
  suggested instead.
